AgentMeter {{VERSION}} for macOS (build {{BUILD}}).

## What's new

- Added Google Antigravity personal-plan quota monitoring. Enable Google Antigravity under Settings → Plan after signing in with the standalone Antigravity CLI.
- View remaining quota and reset times by model group, including the five-hour and weekly buckets returned by the CLI. Settings search also recognizes Gemini CLI.
- Allow more time for quota queries on slower connections.
- Sync sanitized Antigravity quota snapshots through private iCloud for compatible iPhone and Apple Watch clients. Collection pause, stale-data handling, offline retries, and iCloud account changes are supported.

## Validation and compatibility

- Release artifacts use Production CloudKit with the existing QuotaSnapshot record type.
- iPhone, widgets, and Watch require a client version that recognizes Antigravity quota snapshots. Exact TestFlight and physical Watch end-to-end validation remains pending.
- Real-account Perplexity Auto/Manual comparison and Volcengine IAM/usage checks remain pending.
- Provider credentials stay on the collecting device.
